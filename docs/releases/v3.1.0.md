A new look for every screen. unstrap 3.1 is built on Halo: solid cards, one light per screen, the numbers in dots, and pages that hold still while the day's data loads. It also speaks Spanish and measures in miles.

## Today

- **Today follows the day.** Waking up, midday, workout time, after a workout, evening and night each have their own hero and cards.
- **Hold a card to pin it or hide it,** and drag pinned cards into the order you want.
- Today shows the day as loading until its numbers arrive, instead of the first-night cards.
- An empty day says nothing has been recorded yet, rather than judging it.
- Last night is lit in sleep blue, and never two lit cards sit in a row.
- A partial day says so on the cards it affects.

## Details

- **Every detail page has one frame.** The number, its word and the line under it stay where they are as you move between days, and the last day's values stay until the next day's arrive.
- Each kind of reading has its own picture: heart rate as ranges, resting heart rate as nights against your normal, steps against your goal.
- Day, Week, Month and Year cross-fade, and the range control slides.
- Recovery and Sleep, opened from Today, open their detail pages.
- Resting heart rate, HRV, skin temperature and breathing compare the night shown with your normal, so the word, the number and the figures agree.
- Chart labels never overlap, and every figure carries its unit.

## Workouts

- **Ready has one layout for every sport,** with the goal on a ruler, your zones and saved intervals.
- **Live has one frame for every sport,** with a lit hero, Hold to stop, and the strap's battery.
- Rest is lilac, so it never reads as a heart rate zone.
- Strength counts the set in progress, keeps Name it during rest, marks the exercise you picked, and says when a set was skipped.
- **Route map.** Open a run's route full screen, follow it on roads or as recorded, pick a kilometre or a mile to see it on the map, and share it as GPX.
- Summaries for every kind of workout, with a share picture.

## Miles

- **Choose miles in your profile's Units row,** and every distance, pace, speed and split follows: Ready goals, Live, summaries, the route map, widgets, share pictures and the workout notification. Height reads in feet and inches.

## Widgets

- Five widgets in Halo: Moment, Recovery, Steps, Sleep and Start, each at three sizes on one fixed grid.
- While a workout records, Moment and Start show it live.
- Moment always draws, even while the day is still being read.

## You

- Your profile, goals, heart rate zones, alarms, phone alerts, firmware, backup and Health Connect, as plain lists with picker sheets.
- **Goals and bests, all badges and Your week,** with moments for a new best or a goal met.
- The privacy line says exactly what goes online: update checks on GitHub, maps from OpenFreeMap when you open a route, and Zepp when you sign in or check firmware.

## Spanish

- **unstrap in Spanish.** Every screen, widget and notification follows your phone's language.

## Smaller things

- Lengths read as "7 h 24" and "1 min 22", never as a clock.
- One name for each thing: Strength, Rowing, Resting heart rate, Your normal, Weekly recap.
- Every button and control has a 48 dp touch area.
- Text stays whole at large font sizes: titles step down a size before they cut off, and cards grow when they need to.
