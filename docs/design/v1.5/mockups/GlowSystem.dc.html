The family rule. Locked on every card: corner radius, grain, hairline edge, the black the core fades to, and where the text sits. Free per card: colour for what it is, light side and height for what it means, strength for its status.

Dots are for numbers. Only the dot-matrix digits are made of dots. Charts use lines, arcs, bars and bands.

Three tiers. Glow card for the main card, edge light card for mid cards with a shape of their own, one grouped list for the small facts.

The glow budget. The top card plus at most one more glows on a screen. Mid cards are edge light cards: near black, with a soft glow coming in from one edge in the metric colour. No bright line on the border, only a hairline. Off your normal, the glow grows and the hairline takes the metric colour.

Where the glow sits. Bottom by default. Top when above your normal, bottom when below. Progress lights the bottom edge only as far as the value. Live cards light the right edge (now), cards about earlier light the left. Sleep lights the top, like a night sky.
