# Text sweep: You page and everything under it

Code read: `openstrap-halo/app/src/main` (Kotlin under `kotlin/org/openstrap/app/`, strings in `res/values/`). Nothing edited.
Width estimates assume a 390 dp phone, page gutter 20 dp, so a panel is 350 dp wide. At 360 dp subtract 30 dp (about 4 characters).

## How the shared slots behave (from PageParts.kt, GlowList.kt)

| Slot | Style | maxLines / overflow | Rough width |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page title (PageHeader) | pageTitle 17 | 1, no overflow set (clips) | about 190 dp, 24 chars |
| Page subtitle | secondary 13 | 1, no overflow set (clips) | about 190 dp, 30 chars |
| Group heading (SectionLabel) | secondary 13, grey | 1, ellipsis | 350 dp |
| Nav row name (RowName) | body 15 | 1, ellipsis | about 220 dp, 28 chars |

Nothing on these pages truncates a nav subtitle, switch subtitle, note or footnote, so long copy there does not get cut, it makes the row tall. What does get cut (clip or ellipsis): page titles, banner titles, sheet titles, nav row names, value-row values past 2 lines, menu row values, the Next badge name, small buttons.

Flag codes: **F** may not fit or makes the row tall, **S** a sentence where a word or phrase belongs, **D** same idea worded differently elsewhere, **G** gap (slot has no text where one is needed, or dead string), **B** bug in how the text is built.

1. **Backup banner note has a double full stop and a lower-case start**: "There isn't enough space for it.. today at 03:12". Built in `BackupPage.kt` by `joinToString(". ")` over strings that already end in a stop. Fix: one join rule (comma or a separate line).

## Style patterns

### Joining parts in one line

Counted over the 826 strings in the You-area files (`strings_you_*`, `strings_update`, `strings_glow_goals`, `strings_glow_celebrate`, `strings_core_rewards`, `strings_workout_zones`, `strings_system_strap`; plural items counted separately):

- **Full stop** between a state and the rest: about 42 strings. Examples: "Off. A zip each day in a folder you choose", "On. Last copied 10:42", "Stopped. Install Health Connect", "Connected. Synced 2 min ago", "Charging. Wear data pauses until it's back on.", "Up to date. Checked today at 19:00", "Last backup failed. <reason>", "Sync stopped. <reason>", "Installing. unstrap closes when it's done", "7 h 30. Your sleep need is 8 h".
- **Comma** between figures and a clause: about 46 strings. Examples: "Max 192 bpm, the same zones for every sport", "185 bpm, set by you", "250 lines, for bug reports", "3 workouts a week, personal bests and badges", "Signed in, used for update checks", "Downloading, 45%", "Download and install, 19.3 MB", "Goal met 6 weeks in a row, your best yet.", "Repeat, Weekdays", "3.25.1, 1,204 KB", "Above 150, below 40", "34 years, male, 72 kg, 178 cm", "8,000 steps, 450 active kcal, 8 active hours".
- **Built in code, not in strings** (cannot be translated or fixed in the XML): `", "` (alarms heading "On the strap, 3 of 10"; profile summary; goals line), `". "` (backup banner), `" "` (updates footnote, and "Image" etc.), `"\n"` (deleted sleep details).
- Rule of thumb in the existing copy: a **status then a detail** uses a full stop, a **figure then a clause** uses a comma. It breaks on "7 h 30. Your sleep need is 8 h" (figure, then full stop) and "Downloading, 45%" (status, then comma). No string uses a dash, a semicolon or a middle dot (rule held).
- "On, last copied 10:42" does not exist: the app uses "On. Last copied 10:42".

### Full stop at the end of a line

- Ending in a stop: 218 of 826. By kind: note and body strings (`_note`, `_text`, `foot`, `intro`) 62 of 74; error and toast strings 41 of 74; status and value lines only 13 of 76.
- So the practice is: **one-fragment status or value lines have no stop; sentences have one**. Exceptions that cause visible mixes: `you_backup_status_off` "Off. A zip each day in a folder you choose" (inner stop, no end stop); `you_backup_restore_note` "Any unstrap zip. Fills in what this phone is missing"; `you_strap_state_charging` "Charging. Wear data pauses until it's back on." (stop at the end in a state line); switch subtitles "Measures while you rest" and "Notifies you of new versions" (no stop) against "Buzzes for notifications that make a sound." and "When off, the strap still buzzes for calls." (stop), all in the same slot type. Toasts: "Couldn't buzz the strap" (none) against "Couldn't open battery settings on this phone." (stop).

### Naming the app and the strap

- "unstrap" appears in 46 of the 826 strings. Lower case always, even at the start of a line ("unstrap 3.0.8 is ready"). The launcher `app_name` is "Unstrap".
- Where it is needed (set against Zepp, GitHub, Android): the Zepp pairing text, "unstrap never sees your password", "unstrap isn't a medical device".
- Where it could go: errors that already sit on an unstrap page ("unstrap can't open that file", "unstrap wasn't allowed to write there", "unstrap no longer has permission"), the About row value ("unstrap 3.0.7"), the Updates footnote ("You have unstrap 2.1.0."), "Any unstrap zip", "That file isn't an unstrap copy", "Updated to unstrap 2.2.0".
- The strap: "the strap" almost everywhere ("the strap didn't answer"), "this strap" for the connected one, "your strap" for the page ("Your strap"), "Helio Strap" in the pairing title and found sheet, "Amazfit Helio Strap" once (`you_zepp_account_other_devices`), "Chinese-market strap" as a switch name. Zepp is "Zepp", "the Zepp app", "Zepp's own page", "Zepp account" (all four).

### Number formats

- Thousands: comma from `Units.count` ("8,000 steps", "1,234 records") but plain in `%d` strings ("Copied 1234 records", "(1234567 bytes)"). Dot-matrix numbers use a thin space (not in these pages).
- Units: with a space ("72 kg", "178 cm", "150 bpm", "19.3 MB", "1,204 KB", "10 s", "-62 dBm", "7 h 30"), without ("86%", "90%", "5′10″", "30%"). "min" in "Every 10 min", "10 min or more", but "10 minutes or more", "20 to 60 minutes a week".
- "of" against "/": "of" in about 14 strings ("3 of 10", "5 of 24 earned", "3 of 4", "8 of 24", "4.2 of 5 km"); "/" in 2 places ("Syncing heart rate (3/12)", the "4/4" on the first-goal badge face). 
- Times: 12 or 24 hour by the phone's clock (`TimeOfDay`); ranges always "22:00 to 07:00" with "to", never a dash (rule held).
- Dates: weekday forms "Tue 20 Oct", "Tuesday", "Thursday, 30 October 2026", "25 Oct 2026", "27 October to 2 November".
- Ages of events: "2 min ago", "just now" (lower-cased in code), "3 h ago", "2 days ago".

### Capitalisation of labels

- All labels, row names, headings, buttons are **sentence case** ("Goals and bests", "Heart rate zones", "Zepp account", "Add sample data", "Stay connected in the background"). Proper names keep caps ("Health Connect", "Zepp", "GitHub", "Helio Strap", "Bluetooth", "Do Not Disturb").
- Exceptions to watch: "OK" (firmware) while others say "Done" or "Close"; "Auth key" (label) against "auth key" (in sentences) fine; "Weekdays" after "Repeat," in the alarm sheet but "weekdays" in "06:40 weekdays" (lower-cased in code); "Full ring"; segmented labels "Single / Double / Triple / Long".
- Group headings are written sentence case but several comments describe them as "capitals' style", so a reader of the code may expect upper case.
